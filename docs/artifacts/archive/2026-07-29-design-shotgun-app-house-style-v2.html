App House Style · second pass

Which kitchen should Keukenbrein become?

The first pass standardized components but left the app visually fragmented. These three directions rebuild the composition, typography, density, and dark theme together. Every frame below uses the same difficult content.

Use the canvasStock and Recipes currently leave most desktop space empty.
Remove visual noiseToo many frames and borders give every region equal weight.
Design dark modeSecondary actions must remain visible without muddy olive-on-olive surfaces.

Compare the three design directions

Direction ARecommended

Soft Utility

Calm, contemporary, and content-led. Repeated household work stays easy to scan without feeling clinical.

StrengthBest daily rhythm and lowest visual maintenance.
Trade-offPurposefully quieter than the other options.
