The design question

Stock already works. The unresolved choice is what should dominate the first screen: food to use, a complete quantity ledger, or household status.

The recommendation keeps the Meals-first model, removes best-before tracking and its manual upkeep, and moves recipe curation behind the actual stock groups.

Recommendation · A. Use Next First
Fixed in every direction
  • Green Ribbon identity
  • Activity + Add stay visible
  • Immediate quantity controls
  • No best-before tracking
  • Search, filters, review, Undo
  • No schema, AI, AH, or Shopping change

A. Use Next First

Identity and page commands stay green. One calm paper deck owns scope, search, outcomes, and Filters. The actionable meal groups precede recipe upkeep.
