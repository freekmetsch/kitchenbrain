Cook, or use the freezer.

That is the whole weekly decision. Cooking already means eating some and freezing the rest. The selected source tells Shopping which ingredients are actually needed.

What freezer targets do They help choose the source. They do not calculate a special batch, change the recipe, or create a third action.

Serve what is already ready

Plan from freezer. Shopping adds only fresh sides such as rice, bread, lime, or garnish. After eating, confirm portions used.

Implementation contract

Every shortlist write persists one existing source: fresh for Cook or freezer for Use freezer. Existing servings, Shopping reconciliation, freeze confirmation, and consume confirmation remain authoritative. No batch calculator, no top-up action, and no inventory mutation at planning time.
