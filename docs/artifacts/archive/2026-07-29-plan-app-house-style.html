Unify meaning, preserve the jobs

The plan is deliberately strict about same-role callers and equally strict about leaving specialized interaction systems alone.

Consolidate completely

  • Stable Stock, Meal plan, Shopping, Recipe, Settings, and Login actions and fields
  • Filters versus passive status as separate semantics
  • Repeated-row frames and meaningful serif section titles
  • Actionable notices with preserved recovery and announcement behavior
  • Recipe card category accent with its textual label
  • Old same-role recipes deleted inside each ticket

Do not flatten

  • Route information architecture or useful green-band payloads
  • Assistant correction files while that plan is in flight
  • Cook Mode timeline, timers, counters, and timer chips
  • Bottom navigation, real segmented tabs, checkboxes, steppers, and drag controls
  • Mutations, URLs, schema, auth, AI, AH, or Dutch lookup fields
  • DaisyUI globally or one universal variant-heavy component

Critique result

Twelve realistic failure paths are documented in the source plan. Every one has a direct mitigation and low residual risk.

The strongest objection

A global DaisyUI restyle would touch fewer files. It would also change unlike roles—Cook Mode, Assistant, navigation, checkboxes, and dialogs—and move the drift into exceptions. Semantic opt-in recipes are the durable choice because they centralize only proven same-role callers, retain native behavior, and allow the old dialect to be deleted.

Highest-risk details are explicitly covered: Dutch/zoom header fit, filter versus status semantics, compact hit targets, dark tonal-field focus, notice severity, and the recipe-only stripe boundary.
