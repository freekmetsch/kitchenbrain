The atmosphere is already right. This review tests five ways to make sections, buttons, fields, pills, and states feel consistently related without flattening every route into the same layout.

Current truth

Unify meaning, not page layout.

Stock, Meal plan, Shopping, and Recipes now share the olive page frame. The remaining drift sits in body-level recipes that are individually reasonable but collectively ambiguous.

Starting recommendation Ledger Grammar plus the six recommended contracts below. It changes the least while giving future components a clear owner.
Keep route-specific composition

Stock can stay ruled, Recipes can stay visual, and Shopping can stay operational. Shared roles—not whole pages—need shared treatments.

Reduce semantic ambiguity

A capsule should not sometimes mean filter, sometimes metadata, and sometimes an action unless its state language makes that distinction unmistakable.

Design for repeated phone use

Maintain 44 px touch targets, but allow compact visual density through inner geometry, type, separators, and invisible hit-area padding.

Decision 0

Choose the base character.

Every specimen contains the same long title, selected filter, status, warning, and action set. Use the state switch to stress the systems equally.
